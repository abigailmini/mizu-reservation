An Anniversary Isn't a Meal.
It's an Evening You'll Remember.

For the nights that matter, a table in a busy dining room won't do. Mizu Omakase gives you an intimate counter, a course progression that unfolds like a story, and the quiet space to actually be together.

When you search for the best anniversary restaurant in PJ, you're really searching for a feeling: intimacy, occasion, and the sense that tonight is different from every other Tuesday. A generic "nice restaurant" gives you a good plate of food. An omakase counter gives you an experience with a beginning, a middle and a crescendo — shared side by side, in a room built for exactly ten people.

That is why Mizu Omakase, tucked into Jaya One in Petaling Jaya, has become a quiet favourite for anniversaries, proposals, and milestone celebrations.

Why an Omakase Counter Is the Perfect Anniversary Setting

Intimacy by design

Only ten seats. No sprawling dining hall, no tables of twelve celebrating a work function beside you. The room stays quiet and warm, and the chef works an arm's length away. It's the difference between eating at a restaurant and being hosted.

A progression, not a plate

An omakase is paced deliberately — each course building on the last, giving you natural pauses to talk, toast, and take it in. The meal has an arc. Anniversaries deserve an arc.

Side by side, not across a table

At the counter you sit shoulder to shoulder, both facing the chef, sharing every reveal in real time. It's more connected than staring at each other over a candle — and far more memorable.

How to Plan the Perfect Anniversary Dinner at Mizu

1

Dinner sittings run 6–8PM and 8:30–10:30PM. The later sitting is quieter and more romantic; the earlier one suits a leisurely start.
