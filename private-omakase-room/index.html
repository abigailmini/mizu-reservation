A Private Omakase Room
for the Moments That Matter

Close a deal, celebrate a milestone, or gather the people who matter — in a cozy, exclusive room where the chef cooks for your table alone. Private omakase in the heart of Petaling Jaya.

Some occasions deserve more than a booked table in a busy restaurant. A signing dinner, a landmark birthday, a family reunion, an intimate proposal — these want privacy, exclusivity and undivided attention. That's exactly what Mizu Omakase's private dining option delivers: your own space, your own pace, and a chef focused entirely on your group.

If you've been searching for a private omakase room in KL or PJ, here's everything you need to know to host with us.

The Cozy Room

Mizu's private dining space — the Cozy Room — is designed for intimacy, not scale. Warm timber tones, soft lighting, and the same gold-on-brown calligraphy aesthetic that defines Mizu. It seats a small group comfortably and keeps your evening entirely your own: no neighbouring tables, no interruptions, no rush.

It's the sweet spot between a restaurant and a home dinner party — the polish of a chef's counter with the discretion of a private room.

What to Expect

  • An exclusive space for your group alone, for the full session.
  • A chef-led omakase progression — the same craft, aging and seasonal sourcing as our main counter.
  • Flexible courses per guest: mix Mirin, Kirin and the Uni Tasting across your party.
  • Optional add-ons & pairings: sake pairing, extra uni, otoro, A5 wagyu, and more.
  • Special touches on request: tell us the occasion and we'll help tailor the evening.
  • Corkage available at RM200 if you'd like to bring a bottle to mark the day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Mizu have a private dining room?

Yes — the Cozy Room is a private, exclusive space ideal for corporate dinners, birthdays and celebrations at Jaya One, PJ.

How do I book a private omakase event?

WhatsApp 012-913 0952 with your date, group size and occasion, and we'll arrange everything.

Can we bring our own wine or sake?

Yes, corkage is RM200 per bottle. Sake pairing is also available as an add-on.

Are children allowed at private events?

Children under 6 are not permitted to preserve the experience. Guests 6 and above are welcome and order a full course.
